# Paula Luruena Marquez — Consumer & Brand portfolio

Portfolio of Paula Luruena Marquez, Tulane psychology senior. Research, clinical, community, and business experience, applied to how people choose, trust, and connect with what they buy.

Live site: https://paulaluruena.github.io/Paula_Luruena_Portfolio_ConsumerBrand/

It uses all of her experience and gives more weight to its own area. Psychology is the center.

## What is on the page

- **Interests:** consumer insight and fashion, clients and sales, digital and e-commerce.
- **Two linen blazers, two theories of the client:** a teardown of two real product pages.
- **The product page, taken apart:** six things a product page can do to a decision, each with the research behind it, what Paula would test, and where she thinks the line is.
- **Five moments in a client’s decision:** the psychology, the retail metrics, and the method for each.
- **How I listen to a client:** one shop-floor exchange analyzed in five steps.
- **From listening to practice:** Well Suited, an AI app where new associates rehearse difficult client moments, with a sample session and a link to the live app.
- **Two studies I would like to run for a brand:** proposals, with predictions, method, and limits.
- **Psychology & the client:** seven ideas from psychology and what each means in a client conversation.
- **My approach:** five stages, from listening to keeping the relationship.
- **Experience, competencies, background:** every role, filterable, with what carries over to client work.

Professional facts come from Paula's résumé and her earlier portfolios. Career areas are presented as interests, skills still in development are labeled that way, and nothing claims results she has not achieved. Illustrative material (an invented brand, a written-for-the-page conversation) is labeled where it appears.

## Files

- `index.html` — the portfolio
- `depth.html` — deeper work for readers with more time
- `story.html` — "Why psychology", an essay in five parts
- `styles.css`, `script.js` — design and interactions
- `assets/` — fonts (Bodoni Moda, Hanken Grotesk, with licenses), photo, résumé

No build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish with GitHub Pages from `main` / root.
